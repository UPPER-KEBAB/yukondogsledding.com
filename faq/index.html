What does a Yukon dog sledding inquiry include?＋

Send dates, party size, ages, clothing needs, role preference and trail length. WH-MICRO routes the quote from those details and keeps the next step inside the inquiry.

What does dog sledding usually cost?＋

Typical Yukon dog sledding runs CAD 220–750 per person, with private-team, longer and full-day trail requests pricing higher. Quotes for your dates come through the inquiry.

Can I request a private or longer trail day?＋

Yes. Select the premium private-team or full-day trail variant for family groups, private pace, longer trail time or multi-day requests.
