Common Questions

Do I need experience to go dog sledding?

No. Our introductory rides are designed for complete beginners. The muscher handles the dog team while you ride along and enjoy the experience.

How much does a dog sledding tour cost?

Introductory 1-hour rides start from $150 per person. Multi-day expeditions are priced by arrangement. Contact us for current rates.

Is dog sledding safe for children?

Children aged 5 and older can participate in introductory rides with an adult. The muscher controls the team at all times.

What happens if there is not enough snow?

We require adequate snow cover for safe sledding. If conditions are unsuitable, we offer alternative dates or full refund.

Can I bring a camera?

Yes. We recommend a camera with a wrist strap or secure pocket. Photography stops are included on most tours.

Are the dogs well treated?

Absolutely. Our mushers maintain their dogs to the highest welfare standards. The dogs are bred for this work and are eager to run.
