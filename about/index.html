About Us

We work with local Yukon mushers who have spent their lives working with dog teams. Our partners maintain kennels of Alaskan Huskies and mixed-bred sled dogs, all raised and trained in the Yukon. We are committed to the welfare of our dog teams and the preservation of this traditional form of winter travel.
