Dog sledding field guide

Know what the quote includes.

In this guide

PlanningAboutQuestionsEnquiriesSeasonsCostsTrip questions

Compare the same trip

Ask each provider to quote the same dates, party size and experience. A lower headline price is not useful if it leaves out something you need.

Separate the costs

Request the total in CAD, taxes and inclusions. Ask about deposits, payment deadlines and any costs that change with your choices.

Read the exit terms

Check the cancellation deadline, refund method and weather policy. Keep the written quote and agreed terms together.

The conditions

Confirm layers, footwear, minimum age requirements and the operator’s plan for weather or unsuitable trail conditions.

Current pricing
