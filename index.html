Yukon Dog Sledding

Experience the traditional Yukon mode of winter travel — dog sledding.

Dog sledding is one of the most iconic Yukon winter experiences. Feel the power of a dog team as they pull you through snowy trails, learn the commands and the history of this traditional Arctic transport, and understand why Yukoners have relied on dog teams for generations. Our tours range from introductory rides to multi-day expeditions.
