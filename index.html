Before the trail
More questions ↗
Will I drive the sled?＋
Do not assume so. The format, participant requirements and training depend on the operator and the outing.
How can I ask about animal welfare?＋
Ask direct questions about work and rest schedules, veterinary care, living conditions and retirement. Evaluate the answers before choosing an operator.
Is all winter equipment included?＋
Ask for an itemized clothing list and confirm sizes. Bring only what the operator’s current briefing recommends.
