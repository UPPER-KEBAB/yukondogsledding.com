Our Sledding Tours

Our dog sledding tours take place on well-maintained trails outside Whitehorse. We work with experienced mushers who maintain healthy, happy dog teams. All equipment is provided — you just need to dress warmly. Tours are suitable for beginners and no prior experience is needed for introductory rides.

What's Included

  • Experienced mushers with well-cared-for dog teams
  • All sledding equipment provided — snowshoes, boots, parkas available
  • Small group sizes for a personal experience
  • Trail options from gentle to challenging
  • Multi-day expeditions to remote Yukon cabins
  • Winter-only operation during peak snow conditions
  • Suitable for ages 5+ on introductory rides
